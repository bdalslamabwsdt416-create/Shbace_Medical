## EB-paediatrics-nephrology-accepted-CH06-Q002 — CH06-Q002 — Q2: Average daily insensible fluid loss

**Source:** Question-bank MD page 86 · Printed page 160 · Answer PDF page 91 · Original question Q2

**Initial explanation**

Insensible water loss occurs through skin and respiratory tract and is conventionally estimated relative to body surface area. A commonly taught pediatric estimate is approximately 400 mL/m2/day, although it changes with age, temperature, humidity, fever, respiratory rate and skin integrity. The unit per square metre is important: a per-kilogram figure is not interchangeable.
