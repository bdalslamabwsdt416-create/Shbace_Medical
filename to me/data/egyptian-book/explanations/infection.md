## EB-paediatrics-infection-accepted-Q1 — Q1 — Bacterial infection that does not produce an exanthem

**Source:** `MCQ د عرفة متقسم` · PDF-sequence page 52 · printed page 84 · answer-key page 67 · original question 1.

**Initial explanation**

Diphtheria is primarily a localized toxin-mediated respiratory infection and does not classically produce a generalized infectious exanthem. Typhoid may produce rose spots; scarlet fever produces a diffuse sandpaper rash; meningococcemia may produce petechiae or purpura.

**Detailed explanation**

Diphtheria is primarily a localized toxin-mediated respiratory infection and does not classically produce a generalized infectious exanthem. Typhoid may produce rose spots; scarlet fever produces a diffuse sandpaper rash; meningococcemia may produce petechiae or purpura.

Curriculum alignment: Infectious 2024: bacterial diseases and systemic infection; scarlet fever/typhoid material, p.13–16.

High-yield point: A petechial or purpuric rash with fever is a medical emergency because meningococcemia must be considered.
