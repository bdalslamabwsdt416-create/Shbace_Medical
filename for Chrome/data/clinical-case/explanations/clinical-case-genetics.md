## CC-0014 — Down syndrome & chromosomal disorders

**Question:** The mother of a 4 months old boy complains her child still cant support his head.
On examination the child has a flat occiput, and a transverse plamar crease.local
examination of the heart shows a hollow systolic murmur over the left parasternal
area. One of the common complications of this condition is:
1. ITP
2. G6PD deficiency
3. Leukemia
4. Pyloric stensis

**Model answer:** C — Leukemia.

Clinical explanation:
The flat occiput, transverse palmar crease and congenital heart murmur suggest Down syndrome (trisomy 21). Children with Down syndrome have an increased risk of acute leukemia, including acute lymphoblastic and acute megakaryoblastic leukemia.

Options analysis:
1 (ITP): not the characteristic increased malignancy association. 2 (G6PD deficiency): unrelated to trisomy 21. 3 (Leukemia): correct. 4 (Pyloric stenosis): not a recognized common association.

Curriculum alignment:
SHETS: Genetics; chromosomal disorders and Down syndrome.

High-Yield:
A child with Down syndrome and new pallor, bruising, bone pain or organomegaly needs prompt hematologic assessment.

support his head. On examination the child has a flat occiput, and a
